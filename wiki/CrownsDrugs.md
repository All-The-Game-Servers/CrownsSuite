# CrownsDrugs

CrownsDrugs is an arcade-style criminal business loop built on CrownsEconomy.

## Main Systems

- Buy seeds and supplies
- Upgrade grow and processing equipment
- Unlock stronger product paths
- Grow raw stock
- Process packages
- Use products for effects
- Sell to black-market buyers for Crowns

## Product Paths

- Marijuana
- Cocaine
- Meth
