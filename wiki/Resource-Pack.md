# Resource Pack

Crowns Suite ships with a Dark Arcane resource pack that gives the suite a shared visual identity.

## What It Covers

- Suite home and navigation icons
- Economy hub icons
- Admin hub icons
- Event relics and rewards
- Drugs item families
