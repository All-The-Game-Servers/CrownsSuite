# CrownsAdmin

CrownsAdmin is the staff and moderation layer for Crowns Suite.

## Main Systems

- Staff tools dashboard
- Reports
- Player inspection
- Inventory and ender chest viewing
- Vanish
- Staff Mode
- Freeze, mute, warn, kick, ban
- Analytics and playtime shortcuts
