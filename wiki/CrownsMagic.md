# CrownsMagic

`CrownsMagic 0.1.0` is the first relaunch gameplay plugin.

## Core Loop

- Players use gesture inputs to cast bound spells.
- Spells consume mana.
- Cooldowns prevent spam.
- Particle effects carry the fantasy identity.
- The spellbook GUI lets players view and bind starter spells.

## Starter Direction

The first design target is glamorous, readable magic: stars, glints, ember bolts, wards, healing particles, and movement effects that feel distinct from vanilla combat.
